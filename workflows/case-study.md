# Case Study Workflow

Use this workflow after a real Builder project reaches a meaningful milestone.

## 1. Freeze the evidence

Record the exact implemented milestone, commit or release, environment, testnet/mainnet context, and verification evidence.

## 2. Separate facts from plans

Document what is actually implemented separately from future scope, ideas, and stretch goals.

## 3. Record provenance

Identify official samples, forks, copied patterns, dependencies, and original work. Do not claim inherited implementation as original.

## 4. Extract decisions

Capture the important architecture choices and why they were made. Include rejected alternatives when they materially affect future builders.

## 5. Extract failures

For each meaningful failure, record the observed symptom, evidence, confirmed cause, fix, and prevention rule. Do not promote guesses into lessons.

## 6. Promote reusable knowledge

If a discovery applies to future projects, update the shared `lessons/`, `workflows/`, `templates/`, or `ARC_BUILDER_AI_GUIDE.md` rather than leaving it only in the case study.

## 7. Re-check before publishing

Remove secrets and sensitive operational data. Re-open authoritative sources for any mutable Arc/Circle facts. Make sure links and verification evidence are still meaningful.
